---

## How it works

### 1 · Browse

The **All** tab lays out all 1,235 stamps. More load as you scroll. Not sure where to start? Hit **Random**.

### 2 · Find

The search box understands four ways of writing:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `1666` | 軽井沢駅 |
| `軽井沢` | 軽井沢駅 |
| `轻井泽` | 軽井沢駅 |
| `KARUIZAWA` | 軽井沢駅 |

Region chips narrow things down to Hokkaido, East, Central, West, Shikoku or Kyushu.

### 3 · Stamp

Open any stamp and the paper frame is empty. **Press and hold** — a dotted square appears where you pressed. Let go, and the stamp lands there.

How long you hold decides how much ink you get: under 0.2 s is faintest, over 1 s is darkest. Every impression has a slightly different ink depth and tilt, so each one is yours.

Afterwards you can drag the **six-step ink scale** underneath to change the depth at any time.

### 4 · Collect

Everything you stamp lands in **Mine**, filterable by region. The **Stats** tab shows overall progress and how far along you are in each region. The bar at the top always shows how many you have.

### 5 · Nearby

The **Nearby** tab uses your location to list the **16 closest stations**, with straight-line distances — handy for picking a stop on your way somewhere.

---

## Your data

Your stamps, ink depths and location are **stored only in this browser on this device**. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Changing devices or clearing browser data will erase your collection.

## About the artwork

The stamp images come from **railway150.jp**, the official site of JR Group's campaign.

This site is only a viewer — it **stores no images** and loads them straight from the official site. The artwork is copyright of the JR Group companies. Please do not use it commercially.
